\section{An allocation rule}\label{sec:rule}
Let $\theta\in[0,1]$ be a type and let $x(\theta)=\theta$.
It is important to note that the allocation is monotone.
The objective is $u(x,\theta)=-(x-\theta)^2$.
\begin{proposition}\label{prop:optimal}
For each type, the identity allocation uniquely maximizes the objective.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The objective is nonpositive and equals zero exactly when $x=\theta$.
\end{proof}
