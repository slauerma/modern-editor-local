# Modern Editor — a three-minute walkthrough

Updated local layout, 28 September 2026. Synthetic paper; scripted model responses; waits sped up. Narration by ElevenLabs; original music.

**00:00:00 — Your paper. Your decisions.**

Modern Editor brings your source, PDF, and review comments into one place. Open a paper and start writing.

**00:00:07 — Ask for the review you need**

Choose a review template, or give your own instructions. Here, we ask for grammar and clarity, with the smallest local edits. New comments can join the queue as they arrive.

**00:00:18 — Read the reason, then the changes**

Read the reason, then see the changes in their paragraph. Switch to Clean for the proposed wording, or Edit to adjust it.

**00:00:26 — Keep more than one good wording**

Want another wording? Quick alternative adds a saved choice. Switch between versions, or edit one yourself. Asking for an alternative leaves both the source and your selected wording alone.

**00:00:39 — Preview is not acceptance**

Preview compiles a tentative draft and marks the nearby passage in the PDF. Inspect how the suggestion reads on the page, then return to your unchanged draft.

**00:00:49 — Accept, advance, repeat**

Use Shift A to accept and move forward. These three corrections go straight into the draft, without waiting for compilation after each one.

**00:00:58 — Compile once when you are ready**

Compile once to refresh the PDF from the current draft. Acceptance and compilation are separate actions.

**00:01:06 — Accept & compile → inspect → Next**

For an edit you want to check immediately, choose Accept and compile. The editor checks the proposed draft, applies it, and waits while you inspect the PDF. Then choose Next.

**00:01:17 — Ask about the argument**

Codex Side Chat can help with the substance of the paper. Here, we ask why the optimum is unique and whether the distribution matters. The answer refers to the current draft, while the manuscript stays unchanged.

**00:01:30 — Ask about the editor**

Switch to editor help for questions about the application. The conversation makes its scope visible, and the response explains the controls. You can return to the paper conversation whenever you need it.

**00:01:42 — Turn an answer into a suggestion**

A useful answer can become a review comment. Here, a proposed clarification returns to the usual queue, where you can edit, preview, accept, or reject it. Chatting alone never applies the change.

**00:01:55 — A quieter writing surface**

Classic view offers a cream writing surface and a compact suggestion panel. It uses the same draft, saving, and review workflow. Switch back whenever you prefer.

**00:02:06 — The same three columns**

Switch the right-hand PDF pane to Changes PDF. All three columns stay in place. Generation takes time; this wait is shortened.

**00:02:15 — Read revisions in the typeset paper**

This is the LaTeX diff view. Deleted wording is struck through, and new wording is underlined. Step through the changes in the typeset paper, keeping equations and surrounding prose in view. It is a separate comparison; your manuscript stays clean.

**00:02:31 — Why did this change?**

Press Why to see the recorded reason for a change. The explanation opens below the paper, with the exact source comparison tucked away until you need it.

**00:02:40 — Read the clean result**

Switch to Clean paper to read the revised wording without deletion markup, using the same captured comparison.

**00:02:47 — Inspect the exact source changes**

Text diff shows the exact LaTeX changes. It is also the place to inspect differences that the PDF comparison cannot safely display.

**00:02:56 — Save. Close. Done.**

Save your draft, close the project, and return home.
